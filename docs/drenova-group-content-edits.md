## 10. Sell Page — Marketing Approach Description

**❌ Current:**
The sell page describes generic "Professional Marketing" and "targeted digital campaigns" with no specifics.

**✅ Suggested addition (from PDF's Three-Step Marketing Process & 7-11-4 Rule):**

### Our Three-Step Marketing Process

**1. Mailers** — We let all the neighbours know about your home with a 5-postcard campaign: Coming Soon, Open House, Just Listed, Under Contract, and Sold.

**2. Social** — Long-form video, 3–4 reels, designer-style photos, Facebook ads, Instagram, and YouTube content — all driving traffic to your home's custom website.

**3. Open House** — After seeing your home online, buyers visit in person already highly invested. Open Houses also enlist neighbours to help spread the word.

We follow the **7-11-4 Rule**: studies show consumers need 7 hours of engagement across 11 touchpoints on at least 4 different platforms before making a buying decision. Our immersive strategy is built to hit those numbers for your home.

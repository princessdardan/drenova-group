## Codebase Patterns
- @upstash/redis uses `Redis.fromEnv()` to create client instances (reads UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL and UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN)
- @upstash/redis auto-serializes JSON — no need for JSON.stringify/parse
- KV_LISTINGS_KEY and KV_SYNC_LOG_PREFIX constant names in compliance.ts refer to key-value storage keys generically, not the Vercel KV product
